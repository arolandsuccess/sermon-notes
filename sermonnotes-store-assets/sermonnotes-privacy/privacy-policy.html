Privacy Policy — SermonNotes Studio

Effective Date: June 2, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 2, 2026

SermonNotes Studio ("we," "our," or "us") built the SermonNotes app as a free application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use SermonNotes on Android, iOS, or the web.

By using SermonNotes, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

SermonNotes is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

  • Sermon notes: All notes you write are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to our servers.
  • Bible verses: When you type a verse reference, the app makes a request to a third-party Bible API (bible-api.com) to retrieve the verse text. No personal data is sent in this request — only the verse reference (e.g., "John 3:16").
  • Cached data: Verse text is cached locally on your device to support offline use.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not require account creation or login

3. Third-Party Services

SermonNotes uses the following third-party service solely to retrieve Bible verse text:

  • bible-api.com — a free, open Bible API. When you type a verse reference, a request is sent to this service to fetch the verse. Please review their privacy policy at bible-api.com.

No personal data is included in these requests.

4. Data Storage

All sermon notes and cached verses are stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser or app storage in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

SermonNotes is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from any users, there is no special risk to younger users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
